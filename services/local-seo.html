Local SEO

Local SEO Services in Nepal

Rank in the Google map pack and get found by nearby customers. Serving Kathmandu, Butwal, and Pokhara.

What this actually means

Local SEO services help your business show up when someone nearby searches for what you offer, both in regular Google results and the map pack at the top of the page. For most Nepal-based businesses, this matters more than ranking nationally, since most customers are searching from the same city you're in. I offer local SEO services in Nepal for businesses in Kathmandu, Butwal, Pokhara, and beyond. This starts with your Google Business Profile, since a fully built-out profile is often the difference between showing up in the map pack or not showing up at all. From there, local citations, reviews, and location-based keywords all work together to build trust with both Google and nearby customers.

What's included

  • Google Business Profile setup and optimization, including categories, photos, and posts
  • Local citation building across directories relevant to Nepal
  • Location-based keyword targeting for your city and surrounding areas
  • Review strategy, since review count and recency both affect map pack ranking
  • NAP consistency check, name, address, and phone number matching everywhere online

Why local SEO matters for Nepal-based businesses

A national ranking doesn't help if your customers are all searching "near me" or naming their own city. Local SEO services target that exact behavior. A business in Butwal ranking well in Kathmandu searches gets little value from it. This is why location-based keywords and Google Business Profile work matter more here than generic national SEO.
