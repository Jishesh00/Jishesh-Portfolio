How to pick the right package

A local shop or new business with a handful of pages usually fits Basic well. A business that depends on local customers and wants steady blog content fits Standard best, since that's where local SEO gets added. A business chasing competitive keywords, or one that wants to scale content output fast, needs Premium, since that's the only tier with priority fixes and a monthly strategy call. If you're not sure which one fits, the free audit will tell you exactly where your site stands before you commit to any package.

What a one-time SEO audit covers

Some businesses aren't ready for a monthly package yet and just want a starting point. A standalone audit checks technical health, on-page content, and local SEO signals, and gives you a prioritized list of fixes. This can be a first step before choosing a package, or a way to fix the basics yourself.

FAQs

Can I switch packages later?
Yes, many business clients start on Basic or Standard and move up once they see results or want to compete for more keywords. There's no lock-in contract forcing you to stay on one tier.
What happens after the free audit?
You'll get a prioritized list of what needs fixing and a recommendation on which package fits, based on your site's size and goals, not a generic sales pitch.
Do all packages include local SEO?
Local SEO is included from Standard and Premium. Basic covers on-page and technical fixes only, since smaller sites at that stage usually need the foundation fixed first. See local SEO services for details.
